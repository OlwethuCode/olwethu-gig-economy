04 / INTERPRETATION & CONCLUSION

Flexibility attracts.
Stability still matters.

In the simulated sample, awareness was 70%, and 65% said they would consider gig work. Flexible working hours was the most selected advantage, while unstable income was the most selected disadvantage. These results suggest flexibility may appeal to young people, but financial uncertainty remains a concern.

Because the dataset is simulated and contains only 20 records, it cannot be used to make claims about all young people in South Africa.
