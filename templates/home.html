01PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding work
in the digital age.

The gig economy is a way of working in which people earn money by completing individual tasks, short-term jobs or projects. Digital platforms can connect clients with workers who offer services such as graphic design, writing, editing, programming, delivery and transport.

This investigation considers the opportunities created by platform-based work as well as challenges such as irregular income, competition and limited employment protections.

PROJECT SUMMARY

Abstract

This project investigates the gig economy, with a focus on opportunities and challenges relevant to South Africa. It explains how digital platforms connect clients and workers, examines flexibility, income uncertainty and worker protection, and presents a career example in freelance graphic design.

A questionnaire dataset of 20 records demonstrates data capture and analysis. The questionnaire results are simulated and are not real survey findings. The project concludes that gig work may offer flexible ways to earn income, but people should assess platform rules, payment conditions, safety and their own skills before making decisions.
